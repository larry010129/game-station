Currency icons: Credits, tickets and fragments

Credits, the Regular and Special Draw Ticket, and the Regular and Special fragment that replaces the crystal, drawn as shaded game art. Each option is shown large, then at 44, 24 and 16 pixels, then in the places it ships. The first block in each set is the line mark in use today, in the blue and gold that ship now.

Credits

Back to basics: five takes on coins and cash, the picture most games use for their everyday currency. Each keeps the plus from the mark in use today. The art has no colour of its own: every tone is mixed from the accent of the theme the user picked, so the shape and shading stay fixed and only the colour follows the theme. Shown here on the default Charcoal theme.
