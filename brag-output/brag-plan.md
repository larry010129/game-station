# Draw Theme: a painter makes your theme (concept video)

**Angle.** The gacha draw is not a spinning capsule. A small painter character paints the theme you won onto a blank canvas, stroke by stroke, and the finished card drops into your Unlocked themes. This is a concept preview of the animation we would build, not a launch video.

**Hook (0 to 2.7s).** The Draw Theme pop-up opens on a blank paper canvas. The painter hops in with a long brush. Caption: "Every draw is painted by hand."

**Highlights**
1. **The painting (2.7 to 10.2s).** Broad strokes lay the background, then the card, then the "Aa" specimen, then three dabs for the accent, caution and alert colours, then the name. The brush dips into the palette between colours.
2. **The reveal (10.2 to 12.8s).** The painter hops, sparkles burst, a "New look" tag appears. Caption: "A brand new look, painted for you."
3. **Into the collection (12.8 to 16.8s).** The card flies out of the pop-up into the Themes page, the other tiles shift to make room, and Unlocked goes from 30 to 31. Caption: "Added to Unlocked. Yours to keep."

**Punchline (17 to 20s).** "Draw Theme. Every look, painted in." with the painter waving.

**Tone.** Polished and warm. Soft fades, one authored moment (the painting), no hard cuts.

**Visual identity.** The app's own dark glass UI, accent orange, and real tile layout. The painted theme is a made-up palette ("Violet Hour") so it is clearly new. The painter is an original character (teal smock, orange beret).

**Sound.** A soft A minor pad and plucked arpeggio, brush swishes timed to each stroke, a palette bloop on every dip, pops for the dabs, a bell chime on the reveal, a whoosh when the card flies and a settle chime when it lands.
