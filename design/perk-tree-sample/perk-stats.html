Perk stats, explained

Every stat on the perk tree: what it is, which kind, how strong it gets, who carries it, and whether the app applies it yet. Generated from the tree data; do not edit by hand.

How stats work

Active

Always on and added up. They sit under the header for what they boost (Income, Season Pass XP, Tickets, Fragments, Streaks).

Conditional

Only apply while a condition holds, like "sessions over 2 hours" or "while GPU load stays under 90%". Listed one by one; never added into the totals above.

Rules

On/off effects: Season Keys, Specials, Pentagons and the grand keystones. They change how something works instead of adding a number.

Percent and flat

Percent stats add up with each other, flat stats with each other, and the two stay apart: +397% +35. A negative total shows in red.

Trade-offs

A notable can give a stronger bonus and take a smaller one away on another theme. The downside is smaller than the gain, never on the same theme, and never makes a total drop below base. It only counts while its gain is in force.

Bands I to V

The outer notables come in five bands. Each band is a stronger copy of the one before: about ×1.5, ×2, ×2.5, ×3 of band I, with its downside growing the same way.

In the app

"Live" means the app applies it today. The rest can be taken (they are the way through the tree) but do nothing yet.
