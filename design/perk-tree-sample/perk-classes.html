Starting classes

Your first 2 paths out of Station Core become your starting classes. Bands IV and V open only in those two.

Costs

Small circle 1 point, notable 2, grand keystone 3. Squares and pentagons are free but limited by slots. Path cost is the cheapest way to reach a keystone.

Band VI

Past the band V keystone: three branches (2 small, 1 mid notable, 2 small) that all end on one keystone. Every class has one, but you can take only ONE branch in only ONE class: the first band VI node closes the other branches and the other classes, and the monthly reset frees the choice.

Bands

Each class has five bands. Band I notables sit near the core; the outer ones repeat as bands II and III, stronger each time. Bands IV and V are unique to the class, and band VI is the one-class summit.
