Classes as constellations: 42 options

A preview, not the tree. Each card is one class, two bands, laid out as a star constellation in the night sky (zodiac-inspired stick figures, not exact star charts). The brightest stars are the keystones, the warm stars the outer notables, and each wheel is a star ringed by six faint ones. The faint lines are the paths. The first 10 are zodiac signs, then Aquarius and Pisces finish the zodiac, then 30 more famous constellations. With 8 classes, pick 8, one per class.
